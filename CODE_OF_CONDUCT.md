# Code of Conduct

Dayspring is built for families and everyday people, and we want working on it to feel the same way: welcoming, patient and kind.

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect
- Be kind and assume good intent. Many people here are not programmers, so explain without talking down.
- Give and receive feedback graciously. Critique ideas, not people.
- Respect people's privacy. Never post someone's personal information, and remove personal details from logs and screenshots before sharing.
- Focus on what's best for the people who use Dayspring.

## What's not okay
- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or family setting.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful. Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact on the repository's profile, or with GitHub's "Report" option. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
