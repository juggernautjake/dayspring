# Personality

Dayspring normally sounds friendly, clear and helpful. In **Settings → Personality** you can give it a character, fine-tune how it talks, or describe your own. Times, alarms and other facts always stay clear, whatever the personality.

## Pick a character

Choose a card: Cowboy, Broski, Fair Maiden, Noble Knight, Star Sage, Groovy, Professor, Movie Buff, Corrupt Politician (harmless satire), Commanding Officer, Pirate, Butler, Grandma / Grandpa, Robot, Noir Detective, Coach, Wizard, Game Show Host, Zen Monk or Bard.

Each character has its own sliders, for example:
- **Cowboy:** Lawman ↔ Outlaw.
- **Noble Knight:** Chipper squire ↔ Brooding champion.
- **Pirate:** Swashbuckling hero ↔ Scurvy scallywag.

Hover over either end of a slider to see what it does. A character may suggest a matching voice. It only switches if you say yes.

Every character has a lot to say. Each has dozens of lines of its own, and they change with its sliders: a brooding knight and a chipper knight sound like two different people. They also say hello and goodbye differently in the morning, afternoon, evening and at night, and react to good news and bad news in their own way. When an AI is connected, each character also has favourite topics and words it likes to use.

## Fine-tune

The twelve sliders work with any character: Warmth, Humour, Bite, Praise, Care style, Maturity, Formality, Length, Curiosity, Energy, Outlook and Flavour. The middle of each slider is "normal".

Moving one slider gently nudges related ones. For example, more warmth means a little less bite. Press 🔒 to pin a slider so nothing nudges it.

The **Preview** box shows how Dayspring will sound as you go. When Humour is at 60 or more, Dayspring offers a joke now and then.

## Describe your own

Write up to 300 words, such as "a grumpy lighthouse keeper who secretly loves people", then press **Build persona**:
- **With an AI key:** Dayspring builds a full persona, with speaking style, sample lines and 2–4 sliders of its own.
- **Without one:** it sets the sliders from your words. The rest comes alive once you add an AI key.

## Save your favourites

Press **Save as…** to keep the current setup, including the character, every slider and your description. You can keep up to 50. On each saved card you can:
- rename it
- duplicate it
- delete it
- set it as your default

You can also export them to a file, and import them again.

## Say it

- "Be a cowboy", "talk like a pirate", "switch to my Lighthouse personality"
- "Be more sassy", "be less formal", "be more brief"
- "What personality are you?", "What personalities are available?"
- "Save this personality as Captain Sam", "rename Captain Sam to Old Salt", "delete my Old Salt personality"
- "Surprise me with a personality"
- "Be normal" or "drop the act" brings back the regular Dayspring.

## Always true

A personality never changes what Dayspring is allowed to do. It never swears, never flirts, never pretends to be a real person, and never encourages anything harmful. Its outlaws, pirates and politicians are all harmless fun. For alarms, timers, health, safety, money and your schedule, Dayspring says the plain fact first.

## Secret characters

There are 15 hidden characters to find. Each one has its own way of talking. You find them by saying the right thing, setting the sliders just so, doing something now and then, or using Dayspring at the right time. Their cards in **Settings → Personality** show "???" and a riddle until you find them.

When you find one, the Dayspring screen sparkles and a card pops up. If the Dayspring window is closed, you'll see a desktop card instead. The notification follows **Settings → Notifications → Secret characters found**; when Dayspring is Off, it's shown but not spoken.

Found characters show their name and picture, but they start locked. Unlock one with XP, which you earn by checking off real tasks. Its card shows the price, and **Settings → Personality** shows how much XP you have. You can also say "unlock the caveman", and Dayspring asks before it spends anything. Unlocking gets its own celebration. The 20 regular characters are always free.

Once unlocked, a secret character works like any other: its own sliders, and you can save it.

- Turn on **Reveal all hints** if you get stuck, or say "give me a hint for a secret character".
- Ask "what secret characters have I found?".
- **Reset discoveries** hides them all again, but anything you unlocked with XP stays unlocked.
