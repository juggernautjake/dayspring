# Photos

Dayspring can bring back memories. Now and then it shows a photo from your computer in its carousel. About once a day, at a relaxed moment, it asks **"What's this one?"** and remembers what you say. Over time it builds a catalogue of your photos in your own words.

## Turn it on

1. Open Settings → **Features** and turn on **Photos**.
2. Under **Photo folders**, click **Add folder** and choose where your photos live, for example `Pictures`, `OneDrive\Pictures` or `D:\Family photos`. Add as many as you like.
3. If you choose no folders, Dayspring uses your **Pictures** folders.

> **Tip:** Make a folder named **Dayspring** inside Pictures and drop your favourites in it. Photos from that folder come up more often.

## What's never shown

- Screenshots
- Anything whose folder or file name looks private: IDs, passports, taxes, bank, medical, insurance, receipts and similar
- Photos you've hidden ("don't show me that one again")

## Things you can say

- "Show me a photo" · "Show me another"
- "What's this photo?": it tells you what you said about it before, or asks
- When it asks "What's this one?", just answer: "That's Sam's graduation in 2019." It files the photo under a category (Family, Friends, Travel, Pets…) with your words.
- "Skip" or "not now" if you don't feel like it
- "Don't show me that one again": hides it for good

## Where the catalogue lives

Your descriptions are saved in `data\photos.json` on your computer. The photos themselves are never copied, moved or uploaded. Dayspring shows them straight from their folders. To start the catalogue over, close Dayspring and delete that file.
