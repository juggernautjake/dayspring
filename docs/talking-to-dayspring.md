# Talking to Dayspring

## The basics

> **No AI brain yet?** Dayspring still understands hundreds of everyday requests (timers, your schedule, recipes, the Bible, math, jokes and more). See [Using Dayspring without AI](using-without-ai.md). When it isn't sure what you meant, it shows a short numbered list: say the number.


1. Say the wake word, then what you want, in one breath: **"Dayspring, what's on tomorrow?"**
2. The ring in the corner of the Dayspring screen changes while it listens, thinks and talks. What it heard appears under the ring.
3. After it answers, you have about **10 seconds** to follow up *without* the wake word ("and Friday?").

Long requests are fine. Dayspring waits for you to finish, even across a few sentences, before it answers.

Your request always comes first. If Dayspring was about to say something on its own (a reminder, a check-in, a question), it waits until your request is done, then tells you. Ask **"what were you going to say?"** to hear it straight away. See [Your requests come first](quiet-and-notifications.md#your-requests-come-first).

### The buttons on the talk panel

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| ✋ **Stop** | Stops talking and listening right away. It waits for the wake word again. |
| ⌨ **Type** | Opens a text box. Typed messages get typed answers, with nothing spoken. |
| 🔇 **Text only** | Turns listening and speaking off entirely. Handy on a call. Click it again to turn talking back on. |
| ↻ | Repeats the last thing it said |
| 💬 **Chat** | Shows the whole conversation, and you can scroll back through it |

You can also say **"stop"**, **"never mind"**, **"repeat that"** or **"text only"**.

> **Tip:** Your wake words are set in Settings → Your assistant. The default is "Dayspring". You can rename it too ("your name is Nova"): see [Renaming your assistant and wake words](renaming-your-assistant.md).

## Things you can say

Commands marked ⚡ work **without an AI brain** (free mode). With an AI brain connected you can say almost anything in your own words. The examples below are just starting points.

### Your schedule

- ⚡ "What's on today?" · "What's on tomorrow?" · "What do I have Friday?"
- ⚡ "Give me the rundown" · "Brief me"
- ⚡ "Add haircut Saturday at 10" · "Add study for 2 hours at 7 pm" · "Add lunch with Sam tomorrow at noon until 1"
- ⚡ "Move gym to 7" · "Move study to tomorrow at 8 pm" · "Push dinner back 30 minutes"
- ⚡ "Remove haircut" · "Cancel dentist on Friday" · "Undo"
- ⚡ "Mark study done" · "I finished the gym"
- ⚡ "Make that major" · "Mark the meeting with Sam as important"
- ⚡ "Do I have anything Saturday at 3?"
- ⚡ "I need to schedule a dentist appointment Friday at 2 for an hour" (it finds room and offers to shuffle things)
- ⚡ "Change of plans, I need to do laundry now for 20 minutes"
- ⚡ "Show me my schedule for Saturday" · "Show my schedule for September the 29th" · "Show me next week's schedule" · "Show me the weekly schedule for November 4th" · "Show me October" · "Show me 2027" · "Close the schedule"

- ⚡ "Do I have any conflicts this week?" · "Fix my conflicts" · "What's clashing with my dentist appointment?" · "Move my study block around my meetings" (checks Google, Outlook and your other calendars too; see [Conflicts](schedule.md#conflicts))

When you ask for a change, Dayspring says **"Okay, I'll make that change."** It makes the change, the schedule updates on screen, and then it confirms what it did.

When you add something that sounds important (a meeting, appointment, interview, flight, wedding…), Dayspring asks **how important** it is: *normal*, *notable*, *important* or *major*. Important items stand out in the week view. Major ones stand out in the month and year views too. Then it offers a reminder: say "30 minutes before", "the night before" or "no thanks".

### Lists, research and results

Lists and research answers open in a **Results** panel. It has a numbered list, ▶ to play a video or song, 🔗 to open a link, and **Open** for a document or plan. It speaks a short answer. Say "read me the list" or press **🔊 Read it to me** for the whole thing, and "close the results" or Esc to close the panel.

- "Show me the top ten most famous Jedi" · "List the best road-trip snacks" (from the AI's own knowledge)
- "Research the best robot vacuums under $300 with videos" (reads several web pages and gives you the links; needs web permission)
- Free mode (no AI): "search for pizza near me" shows a list of web results.
- "Pull up the plans I've made": plans you asked Dayspring to save, plus plan-like documents. Say "save that plan" after planning something together to keep it.

### Your music and video stats

- "What are my most watched YouTube channels this month?" · "What videos have I watched most?"
- "Who are my most listened-to artists?" · "My top songs this year"
- "Find more music like this" · "More videos like this" · "Find songs like Hillsong United"

Video stats come from what Dayspring played, plus your YouTube watch history when the media browser is signed in to YouTube (it only reads the history). Music stats come from Spotify when it's connected. If you connected Spotify before this feature existed, reconnect it once so Dayspring can read your top artists. Without Spotify, stats come from what Dayspring played. Spotify no longer gives apps its own recommendations, so "more like this" searches by the artist's genres.

## Lantern (your courses)

If the free learning app [Lantern](lantern.md) is on your computer:
- "What's my next lesson?" · "How far am I in Python?" · "What courses do I have?"
- "Open Python." · "Open Lantern." · "Install Lantern."
- "Remind me to study Python at 7." (a study block on your schedule, linked to the course)
- "Send the Python course to Sam." (the hub's owner; it asks you to confirm)
- "Add Sam as a friend on Lantern." · "Who are my Lantern friends?"
- When Dayspring tells you about an invitation or a friend request: "yes", "no" or "later".
- "Let Lantern listen." · "Take the mic back." (only one app listens for its name at a time)

## Ask about what's on the screen

Dayspring knows what its screen is showing right now, what's coming up next in the rotating panel, and everything it showed in the last 24 hours: quotes and proverbs, memory verses, the prayer list, weather, photos, recommended videos, pop-ups, reminders and alarms.

- "Read me that proverb." / "What was that quote?" (instant, no AI needed)
- "What's on the screen?"
- "What's coming up next on the screen?"
- "What was that video it recommended this morning?" (with an AI brain)

This history stays on your computer and keeps only the last 24 hours.

## Settings commands

- ⚡ "Open settings" · "Open help"
- ⚡ "Check for updates" (see [Updating](updating.md))
- ⚡ "Show the chat" · "Show our conversation"

> **If it doesn't work:**
> - **It doesn't hear the wake word**: check the mic ("which mic are you using?"), speak a little closer, or add a second wake word in Settings. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#it-doesnt-hear-me).
> - **It cuts you off**: pause less between sentences, or type long requests with ⌨ **Type**.
> - **"Sorry, I couldn't get an answer just then"**: your AI brain couldn't be reached. Free-mode commands still work. See [AI providers](ai-providers.md#if-it-doesnt-work).
