# Your phone (Phone Link)

Dayspring can tell you when a text arrives ("Incoming text from Sam. Want me to read it?") and show app notifications quietly on its screen. It can also draft messages for you. It does this through Microsoft **Phone Link**, which is built into Windows and works with **iPhone** and **Android**.

Dayspring only *reads* what Phone Link shows on your computer. Texts are read aloud only when you say yes, and their words aren't saved to disk.

## Turn it on in Dayspring

Open Settings → **Features** and turn on **Phone**. Then pair your phone as below.

## iPhone

You need Windows 11, Bluetooth on the computer, and iOS 14 or newer.

1. On the computer, turn on Bluetooth: **Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Bluetooth: On**.
2. Press the Windows key, type **Phone Link** and open it.
3. Choose **iPhone**. A QR code appears.
4. On the iPhone, open the **Camera** and point it at the QR code. Tap the link that appears. It opens (or installs) Microsoft's **Link to Windows** app.
5. Follow the pairing steps. Check that the codes on both screens match, and tap **Pair**.
6. On the iPhone, open **Settings → Bluetooth**, tap the **ⓘ** next to your computer's name, and turn on **Show Notifications**, **Sync Contacts** and **Share System Notifications**.
7. On the computer, Phone Link shows your messages. Send yourself a test text and check it appears.

> **Note:** With an iPhone, Phone Link can't show group messages, pictures in texts, or message history from before pairing. That's a Phone Link limit.

## Android

1. On the computer, press the Windows key, type **Phone Link** and open it. Choose **Android**.
2. On the phone, install **Link to Windows** from the Play Store (on many Samsung phones it's already there: pull down the quick settings and tap **Link to Windows**).
3. Open Link to Windows on the phone, tap **Link your phone and PC**, and scan the QR code on the computer.
4. Allow the permissions it asks for: **Notifications**, **Messages**, **Contacts**.
5. On the computer, check your messages appear in Phone Link.

## Make sure Windows shows the notifications

Dayspring sees texts through Windows notifications, so these must be on:

1. **Settings → System → Notifications**: **Notifications** is **On**.
2. In the list below it, **Phone Link** is **On**.
3. **Do not disturb** is off (or Phone Link is allowed through it).

## Things you can say

- "Any new texts?" · "Read my last text" · "Who texted me?" · "What did Sam say?"
- When a text comes in, it asks *"Want me to read it?"*. Say **yes** or **not now**.
- After it reads one: "Reply saying I'll be there at 7" (it drafts the reply)
- "Wish Sam a happy birthday" · "Send Jordan some encouragement" · "Tell Sam I'll be late"

## Sending messages

Dayspring **drafts** messages and reads them back. You can say "change it to…" to reword one. Nothing is ever sent unless you clearly say **yes**.

> **Note:** Sending the text itself (typing it into Phone Link for you) is still being built. For now, after you approve a draft, Dayspring tells you honestly that it can't send yet and keeps the draft so you can send it from your phone.

> **If it doesn't work:**
> - **No "incoming text" announcements**: check the notification settings above, and that Phone Link shows the message on the computer. Dayspring only sees what Phone Link shows.
> - **iPhone disconnects often**: keep the phone within Bluetooth range, and leave Link to Windows running in the background on the iPhone.
> - **Notifications show but not texts (Android)**: in the Link to Windows app on the phone, allow the **Messages** permission.
