# Shopping on Amazon

Ask Dayspring whether something is for sale on Amazon, describe it the way you'd describe it to a friend, and see the listings on the Dayspring screen: pictures, prices, ratings, Prime, delivery. Narrow them down with Amazon's own filters, open one to see everything about it and choose its size or colour, compare a few, look back through your orders, buy something again, and keep an eye on Subscribe & Save.

It's optional and **off until you turn it on**. Shopping is still being built: it's in the development version only (see [Feature status](features-status.md)). It works with **amazon.com (United States)** only, for now.

**Dayspring never buys anything.** It never presses *Place your order*, *Buy Now*, any 1-Click button, *Subscribe* or *Pay*, and it never touches your payment methods, addresses, password, security settings or Prime. Putting something in your cart always asks for your yes first, and "buy now" stops at Amazon's checkout page, shown to you so you can review it and press *Place your order* yourself (or not).

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings → Shopping** (under Apps & connections) and turn on **Shopping on Amazon**.
2. Press **Sign in to Amazon**. Amazon's own sign-in page opens in Dayspring's browser window (the same window YouTube and Spotify sign in to). Sign in there yourself, with your 2-step code if Amazon asks for one. Dayspring never sees, types or keeps your password or codes; it only notices that you're signed in, and says "✓ Signed in to Amazon".
3. A **🛒 Shop** button appears by the clock. You stay signed in, even after restarting Dayspring.

To sign out, press **Sign out** in Settings → Shopping (or say "sign out of Amazon"). That clears Amazon from Dayspring's own browser window only; your own Chrome isn't touched.

## Finding something

Say what you want, with "on Amazon":

- "Find a waterproof work boot size 11 under $120 on Amazon"
- "Is there a 6-outlet smart power strip on Amazon?"
- "Does Amazon sell cast iron skillets under 40 dollars?"
- "Find highly rated running shoes by Ridgeline on Amazon" · "Find the cheapest air fryer on Amazon"
- "Search Amazon for coffee filters" · "Find USB-C cables between $10 and $20 with Prime on Amazon"

Or press **🛒 Shop** and type a description. With an AI brain, Dayspring turns a longer description into a search and the right filters by itself; without one, it understands the usual ways of saying a price ("under $50", "between $20 and $40", "$100 or less"), a brand ("by …", "… brand"), a rating ("4 stars and up", "highly rated"), Prime ("Prime only", "free shipping") and the order ("cheapest", "best rated", "newest", "best selling").

The results are numbered. Each shows the picture, title, price (and the old price when it's discounted), stars and how many ratings, **Prime**, the delivery estimate, and **Sponsored** when Amazon marks a result as an ad.

### Filters

The row under the search box has **price from / to**, **Brand** (with the brands Amazon offers for that search), **Rating**, **Prime** and **Sort** (best match, price low to high or high to low, customer reviews, newest, best sellers). Changing one asks Amazon again with Amazon's own filters, so you see what Amazon really has, not just a trimmed-down page. Your **default filters** (Settings → Shopping) apply to every search unless you say otherwise.

While results are showing you can also say: "only Prime", "under $80", "brand Stormtrek", "4 stars and up", "sort by price", "cheapest first", "clear the filters", "show more".

## Looking at one item

Click a result, or say "open number 2". You see:

- every picture (click the small ones below the big one)
- the price, any deal or discount, whether it's in stock, the delivery estimate, who sells it and where it ships from
- its **choices**: size, colour, style, count… Pick one (click it, or say "size 11", "the gray one", "color navy"). Each choice is its own item on Amazon, so Dayspring reads that choice's own price and stock. One that isn't available is shown crossed out.
- buying options (one-time purchase, Subscribe & Save, other sellers)
- **About this item**, the product details and specs, the rating breakdown (5 stars to 1) and the top reviews

And these buttons:

- **Add to cart**: asks "Add … to your Amazon cart?" first. Press **Yes** or say "yes"; **No** or "no" leaves it.
- **Buy now…**: the same question, then Dayspring puts it in your cart and opens Amazon's checkout page in its browser window with the banner **"Review and press Place order yourself. Dayspring never places orders."** Everything in your cart is on that page. Check it and press *Place your order* yourself, or close it.
- **☆ Save for later**: keeps it in **★ Saved** (on this computer, not on Amazon).
- **↗ Open on Amazon**: shows the item on amazon.com in Dayspring's browser window.
- **☐ Compare**: tick two or three results, then **Compare** (or say "compare number 1 and number 3") for a side-by-side table: price, rating, brand, Prime, stock, delivery and the main specs.

By voice: "add it to my cart", "add number 3 to my cart", "buy it now", "save it for later", "open it on Amazon", "go to checkout".

## Your orders

Say "show my Amazon orders", or open the **📦 Orders** tab: each order's date, items, total, status and **📍 Track package**. Search them with the box, or ask: "when did I last buy coffee filters?" and Dayspring tells you the date.

**Buy it again…** on an item (or "buy coffee filters again", "reorder paper towels") asks first, then puts it in your cart. Then say "go to checkout" when you're ready; you place the order yourself.

## Subscribe & Save

"Show my Subscribe and Save" (or the **🔁 Subscribe & Save** tab) lists your subscriptions with the next delivery date, how often, the quantity and the price.

**Notifications (your choice).** Turn on *Tell me about new subscriptions and upcoming deliveries* (Settings → Shopping, or the switch in the tab, or say "turn on Amazon subscription notifications"). Then once a day, in the daytime, Dayspring looks at your deliveries at a calm pace and tells you about a new subscription and anything arriving in the next 7 days (you can choose 3, 7 or 14), once each, and asks "Want me to skip the October 5 delivery of …?". Answer "yes" to skip just that one delivery, or "no". Turn it off and nothing checks Amazon at all.

**Skip this delivery…** in the tab, or "skip the coffee filters delivery", skips one delivery after your yes. The subscription itself stays; Dayspring never cancels one.

## Your account (read-only)

The **👤 Account** tab (or "show my Amazon account", "am I a Prime member?") shows your Prime membership, your default address's name and city (the street is hidden, and Dayspring never reads it), and your email preferences.

To change something, use the buttons there (or say "open my Amazon addresses", "open my Amazon payment methods"): Amazon's own page opens in Dayspring's browser window and **you** make the change. Dayspring never changes payment methods, addresses, your password, security settings or Prime.

## Robot checks and problems

- **"Enter the characters you see below"**: Amazon sometimes checks that a person is there. Dayspring never answers those checks. It shows the page in its browser window, pauses everything Amazon-related, and the panel says so. Solve it yourself in the window, then press **Continue** (or say "continue shopping").
- **Not signed in**: the panel shows **Sign in to Amazon**. Amazon sometimes asks you to sign in again before checkout; the sign-in opens in the window for you.
- **"Sorry, something went wrong"** from Amazon: try again in a minute; it's on Amazon's side.
- **Too slow**: if Amazon takes more than 30 seconds, Dayspring says so instead of waiting forever. Check the internet connection.
- Amazon changes its pages now and then. If something stops showing, update Dayspring.

## Being gentle with Amazon

Dayspring opens one Amazon page at a time, waits a few seconds between pages (with a little random extra, like a person), and remembers what it just read for a few minutes, so asking the same thing twice is one visit. It never browses Amazon by itself except for the once-a-day Subscribe & Save check you turned on.

To be asked before Dayspring opens Amazon at all, turn on **Ask before opening Amazon pages** in Settings → Shopping. A yes covers the next 15 minutes.

## The window

Shopping is a window like the others: **–** tucks it into the dock along the bottom, **❐** makes it a small window you can drag and resize, **▢** fills the screen inside its margins. Say "minimize shopping", "make shopping small", "close shopping". It always stays inside the margins you set in Settings → Screen.

## Privacy

- Your password, one-time codes and card details are never seen, typed, kept or logged.
- Kept in `data\shopping.json` on this computer: your Shopping settings, whether you're signed in and the first name Amazon greets you with, which subscription deliveries were already announced, and your "Save for later" items. Your orders, subscriptions and account details are only read while you look at them, and are never saved.
- Every Amazon action (searches, items opened, cart questions and answers, checkout pages opened, skips, sign-in) is in the [activity log](permissions.md#the-activity-log), without any secrets.
