Money review read-only

Dayspring only reads. It never sends, pays, transfers, changes settings or cancels anything, and it never types, stores or reads passwords or codes. You sign in yourself.

1. Import a statement (recommended)

Most banks, card sites, Venmo and Cash App let you download your transactions as a CSV or OFX/QFX file. That's the most reliable way, and no website automation is involved. Download one for the months you want (three months is a good start), then drop it here.

2. Read it from the website

Money review is on.

It opens in Dayspring's own money window (its own profile, separate from everything else). Sign in there yourself, including any code the site sends, then press Review. If the site shows a sign-in, code or robot check, Dayspring stops and waits for you.
